The Story

Building from
first principles.

MBA Father of Three Depok, Indonesia YouTube Creator Investor

I'm 37 years old, from Depok, Indonesia. Father of three — Adni, Zahra, and Arash. Husband to Amiroh. My life is defined by the people I love and the work I do in service of community.

My career has taken me through travel, tech startups, property development, and financial consultancy. Each chapter taught me something essential about how businesses are built, how capital flows, and how ordinary people get left out of conversations that directly affect their lives.

That gap — between expert knowledge and everyday understanding — is where Niat Baik Studio was born. It's a Bahasa Indonesia YouTube channel that decodes business, AI, and technology for the curious, the builders, and the everyday professional.

My ikigai: knowledge sharing as service to community. I believe the most powerful thing you can do is take something complex and make it genuinely clear.

Philosophy

Three principles
that guide everything.

01

Reduction

Remove everything that doesn't serve a clear purpose. The most elegant solution is the one with nothing left to take away. This applies equally to business models, content, and design.

02

Integrity

Respect every medium — digital, conversational, or written. Use grids, typography, and contrast properly. Don't cut corners on quality because quality is the point. Truth in execution is everything.

03

Longevity

Build for lasting relevance, not quarterly trends. A piece of knowledge shared well in 2024 should still be valuable in 2034. Work that ages well was built on first principles, not tactics.
